About

Strategy at the top, empathy on the ground.

Over 8 years I've grown from HR execution into a trusted partner for senior leadership — comfortable in the boardroom and on the floor. My work spans talent acquisition, employee engagement, performance, and organisational design across some of Singapore's most demanding environments.
